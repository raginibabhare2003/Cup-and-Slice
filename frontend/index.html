About Us & History

Cup & Slice was founded by three MCA friends from JD College of Engineering & Management as a startup idea. What began as college discussions turned into a shared vision & mission to create a peaceful café for study, relaxation, and quality food. With teamwork, creativity, and an entrepreneurial mindset, we turned our idea into Cup & Slice.

Cup ☕ & Slice 🍕 is not just a café — it’s the beginning of our journey as young entrepreneurs 🚀✨.
